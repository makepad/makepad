# Credits

- `src/kit/analog/`: circuit models written from the published behaviour in the Curtis Electromusic Specialties
  datasheets for the CEM3340/3345 (oscillator), CEM3320 (filter), CEM3330/3335 (amplifier) and CEM3310 (envelope).
  The datasheets are reference only: no text or figures from them are in this repository, and the code is original to
  Makepad and under the crate's licence. The test tables quote their measured figures as facts.
